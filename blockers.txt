StableTrust Integration – Blockers Faced

1. FairyPort WebSocket Disconnects

* FairyPort repeatedly disconnected from BNB Testnet RPC.
* Error: websocket: close 1013: Connection timeout exceeded.
* Required relayer restarts and RPC investigation.

2. Relayer Processing Old Transactions

* FairyPort replayed historical deposits from checkpoint storage.
* Old deposit transactions were repeatedly submitted to Fairyring.
* Required clearing/recreating relayer checkpoints.

3. "pending action" Errors on Deposit

* EVM contract rejected new deposits with:
  "execution reverted: pending action"
* Caused by previous operations not being finalized.

4. Account Creation Callback Failure

* Fairyring account creation succeeded.
* Callback to EVM failed with:
  "execution reverted: no pending"
* Indicated EVM/FairyPort state synchronization issues.

5. Diamond Redeployment & Configuration

* Multiple deployments required.
* Needed to reconfigure token support, operator settings, contract addresses, and frontend environment variables.

6. SDK / Contract Interface Mismatch

* Several expected read functions were unavailable on the deployed Diamond.
* Calls such as getConfidentialAccount() and pendingAction() reverted because the selectors were not exposed through the deployed facets.

7. Balance Synchronization Issues

* Shielded balances were not immediately reflected in the UI.
* Required additional balance polling and synchronization logic.

8. Transfer Settlement Timeout

* Frontend reported:
  "Timeout waiting for transfer to complete."
* Transfer requests reached Fairyring but never successfully settled.

9. Fairyring Transfer Proof Verification Failure (Current Blocker)

* Deposits succeed.
* Account creation succeeds.
* Shielded balances update correctly.
* Confidential transfers fail during verification.
